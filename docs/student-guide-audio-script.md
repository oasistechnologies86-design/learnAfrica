# Learn Africa student guide — master audio script

**Language:** Ghanaian English  
**Recording versions:** Ghanaian male and Ghanaian female  
**Recording rule:** Both versions must read this exact script, word for word. Do not shorten, paraphrase, or change the order. Only the speaker's voice changes.  
**Suggested delivery:** Warm, confident, patient and conversational. Speak clearly at a moderate pace. Avoid exaggerated accents, slang and Twi. Pause briefly between sections.

---

## 1. Welcome to Learn Africa

Hello, and welcome to Learn Africa.

We are happy to have you here. Learn Africa is your online learning space. You can use it to find courses, learn from your instructors, study lessons, watch videos, download learning materials, take tests and examinations, monitor your progress, and earn a certificate when you meet the requirements of a course.

This guide will take you through the main things you need to know as a student. You can listen from beginning to end, or return to a section whenever you need help.

Let us get started.

## 2. Creating your student account and signing in

To begin, create your student account using the student registration option. Enter the information requested and use an email address that you can access.

Your email address is important. Use the same email whenever you sign in. If you already have an account on Learn Africa, sign in instead of creating another account.

After registration, follow any email verification or account instructions shown on the screen. Keep your password private. Do not share it with anyone.

When you return to Learn Africa, open the sign-in page and enter your account details. If you cannot remember your password, use the password recovery option if it is available on the sign-in page.

## 3. Understanding your student dashboard

After signing in, you will arrive at your student dashboard.

Think of the dashboard as your learning home. It helps you find your enrolled courses and see how your learning is progressing.

Take a little time to look around. You may see your course cards, progress information, achievements, and links to other student areas.

If you are not sure where to begin, use the course browsing option to find a course you are interested in. You can also return to the dashboard whenever you want to continue your learning.

## 4. Finding and enrolling in a course

To find a course, open the course browsing area. Look through the available courses and open a course that interests you.

Read the course information before enrolling. Pay attention to the course description, learning content, price if one applies, and any other requirements displayed.

When you are ready, follow the enrolment instructions on the page. If payment is required, complete the payment process using the options provided by Learn Africa.

After successful enrolment, the course should become available in your student learning area. If a course does not appear after enrolment, refresh the page and check your account. If the issue continues, contact the platform support team.

## 5. Opening a course and understanding the course menu

Open one of your enrolled courses to enter the learning area.

The course menu helps you move between the different parts of the course. Depending on how your instructor has arranged the course, you may find sections for lessons, videos, resources, quizzes, tests or examinations.

Choose the item you want to study. Take your time and follow the course in the order recommended by your instructor.

Some course items are learning materials, while others are assessments. For example, opening a quiz or test may first show you an information page. Read that information before choosing to start the assessment.

## 6. Studying custom lessons and reading materials

Some lessons contain written teaching materials. These lessons may include explanations, examples, headings, illustrations and other learning content.

Read the material carefully. Do not rush through a lesson just to increase your progress. Make sure you understand the main ideas before moving on.

If a lesson is long, study it in smaller parts. You can pause, take notes and return to the material when you are ready.

When you have finished a lesson, use the completion control provided in the course. Follow any instructions shown on the screen so that your progress is recorded correctly.

## 7. Watching lesson videos

Your instructor may include videos as part of a lesson. A video may be uploaded directly or embedded from YouTube.

Press the play control to watch. You can pause, continue, adjust the volume and use full-screen mode where your device supports it.

If a video does not load, check your internet connection and try again. If the problem continues, return to the course later or contact support with the course name and lesson title.

Remember that a video is part of your learning material. Watch it carefully and complete any related reading or activities.

## 8. Downloading course resources

Some courses include downloadable resources such as notes, worksheets, documents or other study materials.

Open the resource provided by your instructor and use its download option. The file will usually be saved to your device's downloads area.

On a phone, you may need to open your Files or Downloads application to find it. On a computer, check the Downloads folder in your file manager.

Only download files from the course materials provided through Learn Africa. Keep your study files organised so you can find them easily.

## 9. Understanding quizzes and tests before starting

Your course may include quizzes, lesson tests or other assessments.

Before starting an assessment, open its information page and read the instructions carefully. Check whether it is a lesson test or a final examination.

The information page may explain the time limit, number of attempts, pass mark, question arrangements and any other rules configured for that assessment. If an extra attempt or fee applies, read the displayed information before proceeding.

Do not press Start until you are ready. Starting an assessment may begin its timer or use an attempt, depending on the rules shown.

## 10. Answering and submitting an assessment

When you start, read each question carefully. Select or enter your answer using the controls provided.

If the assessment allows you to move between questions, use the navigation controls to review your answers. Pay attention to any timer displayed on the screen.

When you are satisfied with your answers, use the submit option. If a confirmation message appears, read it before confirming.

Wait for the submission result. Do not close the page or repeatedly press the submit button while the result is being processed.

After submission, review your score and any feedback made available to you.

## 11. Retakes, attempts and assessment rules

Some assessments allow unlimited attempts, while others have a limit set by the course administrator or instructor.

Always check the assessment information page because the rules can differ between courses and between individual tests.

If you have used the allowed attempts, follow the instructions displayed on the page. Do not assume that every assessment has the same retake policy.

If you pass an assessment, your result should be recorded. If you believe your score or attempt count is incorrect, contact support and provide the course name and assessment title.

## 12. Final examinations and certificates

Some courses include a final examination. A final examination may have separate passing requirements from ordinary lesson quizzes.

If your course offers a certificate, read the course requirements carefully. You may need to complete the required lessons and pass the final examination before the certificate becomes available.

After meeting the requirements, open the certificates area in your student profile or use the certificate link shown in your dashboard, where available.

You may be able to view, download or print your certificate. Check that your name is correct before using the certificate.

## 13. Tracking your learning progress

Learn Africa provides progress information to help you understand how much of a course you have completed.

As you study, complete lessons and assessments, your course progress should update according to the platform's completion rules.

If the dashboard and the course page appear to show different progress, refresh the page and check again. If the difference remains, contact support and include the course name and the progress figures you can see.

Do not assume that opening a lesson automatically marks it as complete. Follow the completion instructions shown in the course.

## 14. Your profile and certificates

Your student profile contains your account information and other student options.

Open your profile menu to review the available settings and personal details. Keep your information accurate, especially the name you want shown on course records and certificates.

If you have earned certificates, use the certificates area when it is available to view them.

Always sign out when using a shared or public device. This helps protect your account.

## 15. Getting help when something goes wrong

If a page does not load, first check your internet connection. Refresh the page once and try again.

If a lesson, video, quiz, certificate or other feature still does not work, take note of the course name, the page you were using and the message displayed.

Contact Learn Africa support through the contact options provided on the platform. Explain what happened and include a screenshot if it is helpful. Never send your password to anyone.

## 16. Closing message

You have now completed the Learn Africa student guide.

Remember to study each lesson carefully, read assessment instructions before starting, keep track of your progress and ask for help whenever you need it.

Your learning journey is important. Take it one step at a time, stay consistent and make the most of the learning opportunities available to you.

Thank you for choosing Learn Africa. We wish you a successful learning experience.
