# Learn Africa — Professional Computing: Complete Instructor Setup Guide

This is the working setup guide for building the **Professional Computing** course in Learn Africa. It records the exact lesson titles, curriculum order, custom-page component names, video titles, quiz titles, question counts, question formats, final examination setup and quality checks.

## 2. Required order for every lesson

Each of the eleven topics must follow this exact sequence:

1. **YouTube Video Lesson** — a short introduction to the topic. The instructor selects and uploads or embeds the chosen YouTube video.
2. **Custom Page** — the detailed reading and teaching explanation for that topic.
3. **Quiz Lesson** — the topic assessment containing 50 questions.

Do not insert a separate Text Lesson or practical activity between the Custom Page and Quiz in this revised structure. Practical examples and worked situations belong inside the reading page as teaching examples, not as a separate curriculum item.

The video comes first because it introduces the topic. The custom page follows and teaches the topic in depth. The quiz comes after the reading to assess what the learner has understood.

## 4. How to add each curriculum item

### A. Add the YouTube Video Lesson first

1. Open the course in the instructor curriculum editor.
2. Add a **Video Lesson**.
3. Use the exact video title from the table above.
4. Add the selected YouTube URL using the video URL field.
5. Preview the video and confirm that it plays.
6. Save before adding the Custom Page.

Select a video that actually teaches the named topic. Prefer credible universities, educational institutions, professional bodies or relevant Ghanaian regulators. Check the presenter, accuracy, date, length and learner suitability. The search phrases in the course pack are discovery suggestions, not verified video URLs. Do not treat a YouTube search-results page as the final video link.

For Lesson 11, choose material that specifically explains Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), preferably from the Data Protection Commission or another reliable Ghanaian source. A video about GDPR alone is not a substitute.

### B. Add the Custom Page second

1. Add a **Custom Page** immediately after the video.
2. Enter the exact topic title from the table.
3. Open the custom component selector.
4. Select the matching **Professional Computing 01–11** component.
5. Save and preview it as a student.

The custom page is the detailed teaching material. It should explain the topic in a natural teacher-to-learner voice, not just display headings and short bullet points. Use clear paragraphs, relevant Ghanaian examples, worked situations, useful images or diagrams, learning outcomes and key terms. Keep text readable and left-aligned; avoid narrow columns that stretch spaces or break words awkwardly.

Do not put the YouTube player or quiz inside the custom page. Those are separate curriculum items.

### C. Add the Quiz Lesson third

1. Add a **Quiz Lesson** immediately after the Custom Page.
2. Use the exact quiz title from the table.
3. Add 50 questions for that topic.
4. Select the appropriate question type for every question.
5. Enter the correct answer in the instructor editor.
6. Set the pass mark, timer and attempt policy according to the approved course settings.
7. Save and preview the quiz.

## 5. Lesson quiz specification — 50 questions per topic

Every lesson quiz must contain **exactly 50 questions**. Use the three supported question formats:

| Question type | Questions per lesson quiz |
|---|---:|
| Multiple Choice (MCQ) | 30 |
| True / False | 10 |
| Fill in the Blank | 10 |
| **Total** | **50** |

This produces 550 lesson-quiz questions across the eleven topics.

### Quality requirements for each 50-question bank

- Cover the full reading, not just the first few paragraphs.
- Test definitions, distinctions, principles, examples, practical application and Ghana-focused situations where relevant.
- Include questions across all major subtopics and learning outcomes.
- Use clear language and one unambiguous correct answer.
- MCQ questions should have one correct option and plausible distractors.
- True/False statements should test meaningful understanding, not confusing wording.
- Fill-in-the-blank questions should have a clear expected answer. Keep the answer wording consistent with the lesson and check how the platform marks typed responses.
- Avoid duplicate questions and superficial rewordings.
- Do not make the correct option obvious simply because it is longer or more detailed.
- Check every correct answer against the reading and a reliable source where legal or technical accuracy matters.
- Keep the answer key in the instructor quiz configuration. Do not reveal answers in the student-facing reading page or before quiz submission.

The final examination must assess all eleven topics, not only Lesson 11. Distribute questions across the topics in proportion to their importance and coverage. Include integrated scenarios that require learners to connect more than one topic, especially ethics, professional conduct, cybercrime, privacy, intellectual property, organisational responsibility and Act 843.

Mark this quiz as the **Final Examination** using the existing instructor control. Configure its pass score, timer and attempts according to the approved course policy. Keep the final examination separate from the 11 lesson quizzes.

The overall target is **650 questions**: 550 across the eleven lesson quizzes and 100 in the final examination.

## 7. Question answers and instructor answer key

For every question, the instructor must enter the correct answer in the quiz editor. The answer key must be checked before publishing.

- MCQ: mark the correct option.
- True / False: set the correct truth value.
- Fill in the Blank: enter the expected answer in the answer field and confirm the platform's matching behaviour.

Students should see the questions and submit their responses. Correct answers and explanations should only be revealed according to the course's chosen post-submission feedback settings. Never put the answer key in a student-visible Custom Page.

## 8. Reading content review checklist

Review all eleven custom pages against the expanded Professional Computing course pack before publishing.

Each reading page should include:
- A clear introduction explaining why the topic matters.
- Learning outcomes written in plain language.
- A detailed explanation of every major concept in the topic.
- Definitions and distinctions students are likely to confuse.
- Relevant examples, preferably grounded in Ghanaian education, workplaces, businesses and public services.
- At least one worked situation or case that demonstrates how to apply the ideas.
- Relevant images, diagrams or illustrations that support the explanation.
- A concise key-terms section and a short recap.
- Natural teaching language that guides the learner through the ideas.

Do not pad the reading with repeated statements. Keep paragraphs readable and spacing compact. Images must support the topic and should not be decorative filler.

## 9. Lesson-by-lesson coverage checklist

Use these coverage areas when reviewing each lesson's reading and its 50-question quiz.

1. **An Overview of Ethics:** ethics, morals, values, integrity, law, stakeholders, competing duties, business ethics, corporate social responsibility and ethical decision-making.
2. **Ethics for IT Workers and IT Users:** professional conduct, competence, confidentiality, privileged access, least privilege, conflicts of interest, acceptable use, reporting and accountability.
3. **Computer and Internet Crime:** cybercrime, phishing, social engineering, malware, ransomware, identity theft, unauthorised access, prevention and incident response.
4. **Privacy:** personal data, identifiability, privacy principles, purpose specification, data minimisation, profiling, surveillance, retention, security safeguards and breaches.
5. **Freedom of Expression:** opinion and factual claims, defamation, misinformation, disinformation, anonymity, moderation, harassment and responsible communication.
6. **Intellectual Property and Copyright:** copyright, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, public domain, licences, Creative Commons BY/NC/ND/SA, CC0, copyleft, GNU GPL, plagiarism and infringement.
7. **Software Development:** requirements, design, accessibility, privacy, security, testing, quality assurance, deployment, maintenance, fairness and accountability.
8. **Impact of Information Technology on Productivity and Quality of Life:** productivity, automation, digital divide, education, health, employment, accessibility, wellbeing and environmental impact.
9. **Social Networking:** digital identity, reputation, privacy settings, safe sharing, cyberbullying, misinformation, professional presence and responsible participation.
10. **Ethics of IT Organisations:** governance, organisational culture, accountability, outsourcing, supplier risk, transparency, sustainability and responsible management.
11. **Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843):** scope and purpose, data controller and processor roles, data subject rights, processing principles, security safeguards, registration and compliance responsibilities, and the role of the Data Protection Commission. Check legal statements against the Act and current Commission guidance.

## 10. Final preview and testing

Before publishing, test the complete course from the beginning as a student:

1. Confirm the video appears first in each topic and plays.
2. Confirm the matching custom page follows the video.
3. Confirm the custom page displays the correct topic and component.
4. Check text alignment, paragraph spacing, images, mobile layout and desktop layout.
5. Confirm the correct 50-question quiz follows each custom page.
6. Count the questions in every quiz. Do not publish a lesson quiz with fewer or more than 50.
7. Check that MCQ, True/False and Fill in the Blank questions render and accept answers correctly.
8. Verify the correct answers in the instructor editor and test scoring.
9. Confirm the separate final examination contains exactly 100 questions and is marked as final.
10. Test course completion, attempt limits, retry-fee behaviour, final-exam reset rules and certificate eligibility without changing the existing logic.

## 11. Important existing Learn Africa behaviour

This course setup must use the existing Learn Africa assessment and certificate system. Do not replace or casually modify quiz attempt limits, paid extra attempts, final-exam reset behaviour, pass-score calculations or certificate eligibility. Verify these behaviours with a test learner account before the course is published.

## 13. Content and assessment completion record

The expanded course pack is a source for lesson content and question ideas, but its existing quiz banks are not all complete 50-question banks. Some lessons contain fewer questions, and the older pack includes Fill-in-the-Blank and Scenario formats. The older pack also specifies a 150-question final examination. Those older counts and formats do **not** override the updated course specification in this guide: 50 questions per lesson and a 100-question final examination, using MCQ, True/False and Fill in the Blank only.

Before declaring the course ready, the instructor or course builder must ensure that all 11 complete 50-question banks and the complete 100-question final bank have actually been created, answer keys checked, and questions entered into Learn Africa. This guide records the required names, order, counts, formats and coverage; it does not claim that incomplete source banks have already been expanded or uploaded into the live course.
