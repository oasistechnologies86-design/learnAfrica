# Professional Computing: question-by-question teaching coverage audit

**Audit basis:** the 12 quiz JSON files on the repository's `main` branch, reviewed on 11 November 2026.

## Scope and mapping method

- All 11 topic quizzes are mapped to their matching custom lesson.
- The 50-question cumulative final examination is mapped question-by-question to the most relevant lesson using the tested concept and answer choices. Where a question spans more than one topic, the primary teaching location is listed; related concepts should also be reinforced in the relevant lesson.
- The custom pages contain the original lesson reading, expanded teaching sections, assessment-vocabulary explanations and the additional teaching sections added in this update.
- This is a teaching-coverage map, not a claim that the quiz questions themselves have been rewritten or independently validated for correctness.

## Audit totals

- **Quiz files reviewed:** 12
- **Questions reviewed:** 600
- **Question types:** true_false: 180; mcq: 372; fill_blank: 48
- **Topic lesson mapping:** 50 questions per lesson for Lessons 1–11.
- **Final examination:** 50 questions mapped across Lessons 1–11.
